STAGE 2 PIPELINE REPORT
=======================

Current implementation as of 30 August 2026


PURPOSE
-------

Stage 2 turns fold-scoped Stage 1 evidence into measured pretreatment variables
and held-out causal estimates. It is an outer-cross-fitting workflow: every
decision that can adapt to the data is learned inside one outer-training
partition, frozen, and only then applied to that fold's held-out patients.

The workflow requires the Stage 1 handoff, a stable patient identifier, clinical
text, binary treatment, numeric outcome, and outer/inner split provenance. A
definition-only run may stop before patient extraction and estimation.

Oracle columns never participate in evidence compilation, discovery,
operationalization, extraction, supervision, selection, or fitting. If an
oracle individual effect is available, it is loaded only for post-run
evaluation of already-frozen artifacts.


CURRENT FLOW FOR ONE OUTER FOLD
-------------------------------

1. Compile Stage 1 evidence

   The only supported compiler is semantic_cluster_cards_v2. It applies the
   scientific allowlist, removes exact duplicates while retaining provenance,
   and creates a fold-local semantic-card atlas. It verifies that every frozen
   Stage 1 architecture selected for the run is present. Cards, exact members,
   lineage, and the reduction summary are written under
   stage2/evidence_compilation/.

2. Discover candidates exhaustively

   Every compiled card is sent to primary-model feature discovery. The model
   lists all pretreatment patient-level features supported or implied by the
   supplied evidence. One card may support more than one candidate.

   There is no evidence-community graph, ColBERT candidate router, candidate
   retrieval filter, hard candidate cap, or discovery-time causal-role filter.
   Documentation or artifacts describing selected_candidates.json or a
   candidate-registry ranking refer to an older pipeline and are not current.

3. Merge aliases and operationalize every candidate

   Exact normalized names are coalesced first. The remaining names enter
   repeated merge-only batches: shifted alphabetical batches in early rounds
   and deterministic shuffled batches later. A directive may merge aliases but
   cannot exclude a candidate; every unmentioned candidate passes through.

   Explicit investigator definitions are protected. They cannot be renamed,
   merged with a distinct configured feature, revised, or stripped of their
   configured roles.

   The primary model then receives one canonical candidate and its readable
   supporting evidence. It supplies the value type, unit or categories,
   measurement rule, and missing-value rule. Invalid responses receive bounded
   repair; persistent failure is recorded and uses a conservative ambiguous
   ontology instead of silently dropping the feature.

4. Extract all candidates on outer-training patients

   The extraction model receives exactly one patient per prompt, with bounded
   feature batches and document chunks. Every candidate is extracted before
   statistical selection. Each batch has ordinary input/result/completion
   checkpoints.

   Continuous definitions may preserve a documented categorical or threshold
   value when no exact number exists. Mixed numeric/categorical training values
   receive a training-only harmonization plan. That plan is frozen and applied
   deterministically to later rows; ambiguous mappings become null and are
   audited.

5. Supervise the extraction ontology

   The primary model reviews aggregate training values and validation failures,
   not patient text, treatment, outcomes, roles, metrics, or p-values. It may
   keep or revise the same feature's extraction schema, but may not add, drop,
   split, merge, rename, or role-label features. A revision triggers incremental
   re-extraction only for changed definitions. Supervision is bounded and
   explicit ontologies stay locked.

6. Optionally consolidate equivalent extracted measurements

   selection/candidate_consolidation/ is an unsupervised, outer-training-only
   alias pass. In original-candidate order, an embedding retrieves nearby active
   definitions and Python computes mixed-type pairwise association evidence.
   The model may leave them separate or propose a canonical measurement.

   A replacement is accepted only when every source pair is evaluable and
   clears the configured association threshold (0.85 by default), the sources
   describe the same attribute at the same scope and granularity, and the rule
   is demonstrably information preserving. Sources and output must retain value
   type, unit/category granularity, and missingness. Continuous aliases may only
   coalesce. Categorical aliases may coalesce identical vocabularies or use an
   injective synonymous-category recode.

   Crucially, whenever two aliases are nonmissing on the same training row,
   their numeric values or their canonical recoded categories must agree.
   Conflicting overlaps reject the proposal; source order may not silently win.
   Malformed values that violate a continuous ontology retain the documented
   behavior of being treated as missing before coalescing.

   Accepted latents enter later retrievals immediately. Original columns and
   recursive measurement lineage remain available for audit and held-out
   reconstruction. Disabled or trivial consolidation still writes the report
   referenced by final definitions.

7. Assign roles with group elastic nets and held-out R-loss

   Every inner-training partition fits a logistic group elastic net for
   treatment and a separate group elastic net for marginal outcome. A nominal
   factor's contrasts and missingness indicator form one all-in/all-out group;
   continuous and ordered measurements use standardized score groups.

   A feature selected in at least one inner fold for either nuisance task enters
   the outer-fold confounder union. There is no intersection or vote-frequency
   gate. Both nuisance tasks use that common union.

   Candidate-specific cross-fitted nuisance calibrations feed a
   ridge-stabilized R-learner. Each candidate is scored by the held-out R-loss
   gain from adding all estimable treatment interactions for that candidate.
   The ten largest gains per inner fold are retained by default, even if a gain
   is nonpositive; the deduplicated union (at most 50 for five folds) supplies
   final effect modifiers.

   Large candidate sets run their CPU-bound selector in a loky process. This
   process isolation is safe for CLI, notebook, python -c, and stdin callers and
   does not require the caller to wrap execution in an __main__ guard.

8. Freeze definitions and extract held-out dependencies

   final_definitions.json records selected roles, original measurement
   dependencies, latent states, and consolidation-report provenance. Only the
   dependencies required by selected definitions are extracted on held-out
   notes. Frozen harmonization and latent states are applied without seeing
   held-out outcomes.

9. Estimate effects

   An honest EconML CausalForestDML uses effect modifiers in X and pure
   confounders in W; a dual-role variable appears once in X. If no modifier is
   selected, a constant-effect design preserves the causal-forest estimator.

   The supported nuisance family is cross-validated elastic net: logistic for
   treatment and binary outcome, squared-error for continuous outcome. The
   former strict random-forest runtime-config contract has been retired and is
   not an alternate estimator path. Diagnostics audit configured parameters and
   every fitted EconML nuisance clone, including the selected regularization,
   effective CV folds, and whether its optimizer reached the iteration limit.

   Each held-out row receives propensity, mu0, mu1, AIPW score, conditional
   effect, and available causal-forest uncertainty bounds.

10. Aggregate outer folds

   Exactly one held-out prediction per patient is combined into
   cross_fitted_predictions.csv. The cross-fitted AIPW scores produce the
   reported average treatment effect, standard error, and confidence interval.
   Feature and role stability are summarized across folds.


IMPORTANT OUTPUTS
-----------------

stage2/evidence_compilation/
    Compiled cards, exact members, lineage, and reduction audit.

stage2/outer_NNN/feature_definitions.json
    Operationalized definitions before training extraction.

stage2/outer_NNN/ontology_supervision/
    Aggregate review rounds, incremental re-extraction, and convergence.

stage2/outer_NNN/selection/candidate_consolidation/
    Sequential alias inputs, per-pivot decisions, repair/fallback events,
    report.json, registry.json, and completion marker.

stage2/outer_NNN/selection/elastic_net_selection.json
    Inner-fold nuisance votes, cross-fitted diagnostics, candidate R-loss
    comparisons, selected confounder union, and modifier union.

stage2/outer_NNN/final_definitions.json
    Frozen selected roles and measurement/latent dependencies.

stage2/outer_NNN/estimation/{predictions.csv,diagnostics.json,complete.json}
    Held-out fold predictions and audited fitted estimator details.

stage2/{cross_fitted_predictions.csv,causal_estimate.json,summary.json}
    Cross-fold results.


RESUME AND RESELECTION
----------------------

Scientific leaves use semantic input fingerprints and write completion markers.
Rerunning the same command resumes at the first incomplete or invalidated leaf.
Endpoint URLs and worker counts are transport details; model identities and
scientific settings remain part of the relevant checkpoint decisions.

Use --stage2-reselect for the guarded workflow that preserves post-ontology
definitions and all-candidate training extraction, archives selection and later
artifacts, and recomputes selection, held-out extraction, and estimation. It is
not a general request to reuse scientifically incompatible checkpoints.
